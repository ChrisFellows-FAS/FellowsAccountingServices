Chris Fellows

Founder

With more than three decades of experience, Chris Fellows brings deep, hands on expertise to every facet of accounting. His career spans a broad range of industries and responsibilities, from foundational data entry to strategic leadership as a CFO. Chris is known for his ability to transform accounting operations, by building teams, designing procedures, and creating systems that enhance accuracy, efficiency, and productivity.

A proud graduate of the University of Georgia, Chris earned both his bachelor’s and master’s degrees in accounting. His academic foundation, combined with decades of practical experience, has made him a trusted advisor to business owners and board members. He excels at presenting clear, actionable financial insights that empower leaders to make informed decisions. Chris also acts as a bridge between clients and their audit and tax partners, ensuring smooth communication and strong professional relationships.

When he’s not immersed in financial strategy, Chris enjoys staying active, spending time with loved ones, exploring the outdoors, and celebrating UGA football every fall.

Becky Duncan

Senior Accounting Specialist

Becky Duncan is an accounting and governance leader with over a decade of experience driving healthy, audit-ready balance sheet operations within complex, global organizations. Specializing in SOX-compliant internal controls, statutory reporting, and large-scale accounting operations, she most recently served as an Accounting Manager, where she led global governance programs and oversaw critical financial platforms. Her extensive background also includes managing compliance, complex auditing, and technical financial analysis. Known for her collaborative leadership and clear executive communication, Becky is a trusted partner to senior leadership and auditors, adept at leveraging data and automation to deliver actionable insights, scalable governance models, and sustainable process improvements.

Outside of her love for accounting, Becky is an avid runner and a dedicated community volunteer who passionately supports local foster youth and animal rescues. She enjoys spending her downtime with her husband and two daughters.

Steven Fellows

Steven Fellows is a finance and accounting professional based in Atlanta. He currently serves in corporate accounting for a mid-sized residential asset management firm while moonlighting as a food & beverage manufacturing consultant. Steven and his wife enjoy spending time with family, music, and nature.
